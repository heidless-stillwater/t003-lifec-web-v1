# **App Name**: T003-lifeC-web-v1

## Core Features:

- Homepage Sections: Display a homepage featuring sections for 'About,' 'Services,' 'Pricing,' a Call-to-Action (CTA), and AI Pro Tips, offering a comprehensive overview of the life coach's offerings and insights.
- Sticky Header: Implement a sticky header that includes the logo, site name, navigation links, telephone number, and a 'Start Here' button that navigates to the contact form.
- Pricing Page: Showcase a listing of life coaching packages: Personal Growth, Career Transition, Relationship, Mindfulness & Stress Management, Leadership & Performance, and Custom Programs.
- Multi-Page Structure: Develop individual pages for 'About,' 'Services,' 'Testimonials,' and 'Contact,' each designed to provide detailed and engaging content.
- Testimonials: Showcase a selection of client testimonials on a dedicated page, to highlight the life coach's successes and positive impact.
- Information Display: Info Display: Displays contact information, including website, email, address, phone number.
- AI Motivational Tips: AI Pro Tips: LLM tool recommends personalized daily motivational message based on the individual's indicated preferences and needs. It acts as a lead-magnet and provides unique content that keeps people returning to the site.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft sky blue (#87CEEB) to evoke trust and openness, without being a teal cliche.
- Background color: Light, desaturated blue (#E0F4FF) for a calm, professional backdrop.
- Accent color: Warm peach (#FFDAB9) to highlight calls to action and important information, differentiating them from the primary.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a modern, friendly, readable feel in both headlines and body text.
- Use clean, professional icons relevant to each service, creating a consistent visual language.
- Employ a clear, well-structured layout with ample white space to ensure readability and easy navigation.
- Incorporate subtle, smooth animations to enhance user engagement without being distracting.
